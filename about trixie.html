I'm in ELX-1A, taking up Industrial Technology major in Electronics. Pero honestly, hindi 'to talaga first choice ko. Akala ko kasi pag Industrial Technology, IT 'yun — puro computers at coding lang. Imagine my surprise nung nalaman ko na may electrical at electronics pala 'to involved. Nag-roleta lang talaga kami kasi ubos na lahat ng ibang courses. Ang natira na lang, Accountancy at Public Administration. Kaso kasi may word na "public" doon, automatic na naisip namin puro public speaking 'yan, ayaw pa naman namin sa public speaking — so dito na lang kami tumakbo. Safe choice lang kunwari. Pero jusko, di ko na alam tong pinasok ko. Dati sobrang active ko sa art. Sumali ako sa competitions, poster making man 'yan or digital art. Pero somewhere along the way, parang nawala lang bigla yung spark ko. Baka balikan ko rin balang araw, who knows. Pastel pink ang favorite color ko. So ayun. Okay na yan.
